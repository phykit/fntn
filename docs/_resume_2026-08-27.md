### What P1 established, since it landed and the rest builds on it

The unexplained ~2.16 bp proportional residual **is the per-share commission**,
and the reason it read as a mystery is a **regime change** rather than a term:
at USD 3,200 the commission is USD 0.37 a side and the USD 1.00 per-order
minimum binds; at USD 64,000 it is USD 7.31 a side and the rate binds. A linear
model fitted across that boundary splits the difference. Solving the USD 64,000
reading **alone** gives *p* ≈ **USD 43.79** fixed / **USD 31.16** tiered, and the
model then predicts **18.84 bp** at USD 3,200 against the ~19 bp recorded,
having never been shown it.

**Row 1's working records no share price at all**, so the *mechanism* is
confirmed and the *share price* is not. That is a third outcome and it is
recorded as itself: the two models collapse to one because the collapse rests
on the mechanism, not on the price.
