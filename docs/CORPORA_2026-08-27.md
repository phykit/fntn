## The refusal, which is the boundary working

```
REFUSED  buyback_modernisation_form_sr.txt  (adopted 2023-05-03, not before 2023-01-01)
```

**The 2023 buyback disclosure modernisation postdates `archive_opens` and is
refused before it is fetched.** *The boundary check runs ahead of `curl`, so the
URL is never called: material that could not be admitted is not even
retrieved.* **`pre_archive` stays a fact rather than a label.**

## The removal, and the floor was RIGHT

`rule_13d7_dissemination.txt` extracted to **236 bytes**, below the 500-byte
floor, and the integrity check refused the corpus.

***The tempting reading was that 240.13d-7 is a genuinely short cross-reference
rule and the floor a false positive. It was checked, and it is not.*** The
extracted content is:

```
| Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (e-CFR) | US Law | LII / ...
Please help us improve our site!
×
240.13d-7
CFR Toolbox ...
```

***Navigation furniture. The rule text did not survive extraction at all.***
The raw page is 23,641 bytes — a full Cornell page, not the 698-byte stub class
of `docs/CORRECTIONS.md` A1 — so **the fetch succeeded and the extraction
failed**, which is a different failure and the floor caught it either way.

**The document is removed, both manifests updated, and the corpus re-checked.**
*The floor is not bent per-document: a floor with an exception is not a floor.*

## One gap the corpus does NOT close, recorded so it is not mistaken for closed

***`form_8k.txt` is 17 CFR 249.308, which is the CFR section that ADOPTS Form
8-K. It is not Form 8-K.*** Its 868 bytes are a cross-reference and one sentence
about Regulation FD.

> ***So the `earnings_event` corpus does NOT contain Item 2.02's own
> requirements.***

**Item 2.02's text lives in the FORM, and forms live on `sec.gov`**, which is
**fenced behind `SEC_CONTACT`** and which A1 records serving a **698-byte stub**
to `curl`. **The corpus covers Regulation FD, Regulation G, Item 10(e) and Rule
13a-11 — the disclosure regime AROUND Item 2.02 — and not the item itself.**

*That is the same dependency phase 2 hit and phase 3 hit: one environment
variable, three times.*

## No fourth family was added, and the reason is §0.6-shaped

5b permits adding one. **It is declined.**

**P126 recorded a cost of retiring insider dealing that bears directly on this:
the insider family was the one with paper-sourced effect sizes, and the three
survivors have no documented effect in this paper.** **Adding a fourth would
widen a discoverable population whose evidence base is already thinner than the
retired family's**, and it would do so **on the judgement that "the disclosure
regime obviously supports it"**, which is a judgement about disclosure and not
about edge.

*The register is not short of families. It is short of measurements.*
