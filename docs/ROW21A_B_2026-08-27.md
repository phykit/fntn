## 8b. ***REFUSED, and the reason is one this project established one batch ago***

### The identical move was examined and rejected at P120, in this row

**Phase 4 of the previous batch asked whether §14's count of 200 and row 21a's
withdrawn `n` of 200 were the same 200.** The answer was recorded as:

> *"Not the same unit, not the same population, and after P115 not even the same
> pipeline. And the reason is stronger than the mismatch: row 21a is blocked for
> want of a **precision target**; §14's 200 comes from a **stopping rule**; a
> count saying when tracing has found enough says nothing about how precisely a
> rate must be known."*

***§9.4's block size of 100 is the same object as §14's 200: it IS §14's 200,
halved.*** §14's count **is** two blocks of `n` = 100. **So adopting 100 here
adopts exactly the quantity P120 established cannot supply what this row
lacks**, one batch later and at half the size.

| | §9.4's `n` = 100 | Row 21a's `n` |
|---|---|---|
| **Unit** | **items** | **drawn class-level mechanisms** |
| **Population** | the §3.5 item pipeline's live feed | the discovery layer's drawn arm |
| **What it fixes** | when tracing has found enough | how precisely a **rate** must be known |
| **Derived from** | a **stopping rule**, whose block size P120 recorded was *argued from feasibility rather than from power* | a **power requirement** that does not exist |

### The distinction between this and the four derivations that worked

***The four that worked borrowed a quantity that CAUSES the thing derived. This
one borrows a quantity that merely shares a name.***

| Derivation | The borrowed quantity | Why it is causal |
|---|---|---|
| §4.4's ATR bounds | §6.7's risk budget | the risk budget **is what sizes the position** the bound constrains |
| δₘᵢₙ = 17.0 bp | §5.2.2's cheapest break-even | the break-even **is what an effect must clear** to be actionable |
| §3.6.5's daily cadence | Gate 1's ingestion-lag rule | the lag rule **is what kills a stale item**, so the timetable determines survival |
| The minimum share price | row 1's published schedule | the schedule **is the cost** the price floor is derived from |
| ***Row 21a's `b` from §9.4's block size*** | ***a tracing block size*** | ***nothing.*** **Tracing 100 items does not make 3% the right bound on a fence's false-positive rate.** There is no mechanism connecting them |

**"The register carries one sampling unit rather than two" is a TIDINESS
argument.** *It is a good reason to prefer one number over another once both are
justified. It is not a justification.*

***Taking `n` = 100 on a tidiness argument would be the same guess at half the
size, and the row would have to withdraw it again for the same reason.***

## Can `b` be derived at all today? **No, and the attempt is recorded**

***`P` is the funnel depth, and funnel depth is exactly what §7.1 measures and
has never run.*** **The route closes on the input the row is already blocked
on**, which is confirmation rather than progress.
