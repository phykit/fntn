# Which pipeline is §9.4 written about? **The ITEM pipeline. Tested, not assumed.**

**27 August 2026. Phase 8 of the delegated-authority batch.**

The previous batch's phase 6 inventoried **twelve intake points** and answered
that the Form 4 block would exercise none of them. **The inventory was of the
wrong pipeline.**

---

## 8a. The test, and it is a reading of nouns

**§9.4's own vocabulary settles it. Every load-bearing noun is item-pipeline:**

| §9.4 says | Which pipeline that noun belongs to |
|---|---|
| *"Each **gate** returns one of four states"* | §5's gate stack, Gates 1 to 7, which run over **items** |
| *"the marginal defect rate per hundred **items**"* | items |
| *"it draws from the whole **feed** rather than hand-picked **items**"* | the production feed |
| *"Sampling is stratified across **source class** and **catalyst type**"* | §3.1's source ranks and §3.5's catalyst anchors, both item-side |
| *"a mandatory minimum sample of the primary catalyst family's own live **filing flow**"* | filings |
| *"**General items** find machinery defects"* | items |

**And §0.5 says it in one line**: *"a trace harness (§9.4) that runs real
**items** through the funnel"*.

**The discovery layer has no gates.** It has **twelve intake points**, which are
a different object with a different ordering, a different surface and a
different reason-code family. *§9.4 does not mention an intake point once.*

***So the answer is 8b: §9.4's stopping rule is written about §3.5's ITEM
pipeline. The filing corpus is correctly conceived and was pointed at the wrong
intake.***

### What `trace.py` actually claims, which turns out not to be the error

**`trace.py` does not claim to be §9.4's required exercise.** Its first line
reads *"§9.4 **applied to** §3.7's two ingestion surfaces"*, and it names
exactly what it can measure: **§13 row 21's fence false-positive rate and row
23's abort-position distribution**, both *"facts about the machinery rather than
about the market"*.

**The implementation was honest and the inventory was not.** What went wrong is
that the previous batch answered *"would the Form 4 block exercise any of the
twelve?"* against **the twelve `trace.py` happens to cover**, rather than
against the points §9.4's precondition is about. **The answer it gave was
correct about the wrong set.**

## 8b. The ITEM pipeline's intake points, and what would exercise each

**Eleven, `OBSERVATION_ORDER` in `codes.py`, on the `observation` surface.**

| Pos | Point | What material exercises it | **A Form 4 block?** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `item_source_inaccessible` | an item whose document cannot be retrieved | **Yes, incidentally.** `trace_filings.verify_response` already distinguishes a document from a stub, so a withdrawn or moved filing fires it |
| 2 | `running_document_no_anchor` | a continuously updated article | **No.** A filing is `static` by construction |
| 3 | `anchor_provenance_absent` | an anchor arriving with no `visible_date \| feed_timestamp \| cms_metadata \| inferred` tag | **No, and that is the point.** A Form 4 is regulator-stamped, so its anchor provenance is the strongest available |
| 4 | `observation_anchor_absent` | an item with no `t_pub_observed` | **No.** The acceptance timestamp is on every filing |
| 5 | `ingestion_lag_exceeds_window` | a filing observed late relative to its horizon | **YES, and this is the one that matters.** Form 4s are due within two business days and the lag between filing and observation is exactly what §13 row 15 has no observation of |
| 6 | `extraction_class_suspended` | an extraction class below its per-field accuracy floor | **Only after enough filings to measure a floor.** Not the block; the block is what starts it |
| 7 | `extraction_schema_incomplete` | a filing whose parse omits a mandatory field | **YES.** Form 4 is field-delimited, so this fires on genuinely malformed or unusual filings and on nothing else, which is the cleanest possible signal |
| 8 | `issuer_unresolved` | an issuer that will not resolve to a primary listing | **YES.** Form 4s name issuers by CIK, and the CIK-to-listing map is exactly where this fails |
| 9 | `catalyst_date_corroborated` | a claimed catalyst date with no calendar or regulator-stamped corroboration | **No, inverted.** A Form 4 **is** the regulator-stamped corroboration, so it is the material that makes this point *pass* |
| 10 | `catalyst_duration_below_floor` | a catalyst resolving inside one session | **Yes, for some.** A same-day open-market purchase resolves inside a session |
| 11 | `observation_precedes_registration` | an item dated before its directive's `registered_at` | **No.** It is a property of the *pairing*, not of the material, and needs a registered directive, of which there are zero |

**So the Form 4 block would exercise four of eleven directly (1, 5, 7, 8), a
fifth (10) for some filings, and would make two others (3, 9) pass rather than
fire.** *Against the discovery layer's twelve it exercised none.* **That is the
difference the wrong inventory hid.**

## The direction the import fence did not cover. **CONFIRMED MISSING, and closed.**

`CLAUDE.md`: *agent-origin material may not enter the §3.5 item pipeline; it
would re-base §7.1's headline on an agent-selected population.*

**`assert_import_fence` runs in ONE direction.** It walks `discovery.py`'s
closure and forbids `fntn.gates`, `fntn.data`, `fntn.prices`, `fntn.forward`,
`fntn.graveyard`, `fntn.backtest` and `fntn.book`. **Nothing walked the
reverse.** A gate module that imported `fntn.scanner.discovery` for one
convenience would put agent-selected material one attribute access away from the
population §7.1 measures, **and the existing fence would report clean
throughout.**

**`assert_reverse_import_fence` closes it**, and it returns **three** states, not
two:

| State | When |
|---|---|
| `BREACHED` | a forbidden module reaches the discovery layer |
| `CLEAN` | forbidden modules exist and none reaches it |
| **`NOT_APPLICABLE`** | **none of them exists yet, so there was nothing to walk** |

***Today it returns `NOT_APPLICABLE`, and that is deliberately NOT `CLEAN`.***
The item pipeline does not exist in this tree. **A not-applicable check may
never be read as a pass**, so the state is its own and the test asserts which
one obtains.

**The breach path is tested by building the breach**, with a synthetic
`fntn.gates` that imports the discovery layer, **because a fence first exercised
by the breach it exists against is a fence nobody has tested.**

*Written now so it is in place before the module it guards is, which is the
order this project has twice wished it had used.*

## 8d. **What the trace corpus must CONTAIN.** This is the deliverable.

**Not a count. A specification of strata.** §14 requires the exercise to span
source classes and catalyst types; §13 row 28 fixes the count at **200 items**,
two blocks of 100. **This allocates them.**

### The mandatory stratum: the primary family's own live filing flow

**§9.4 requires it in terms**, and gives the reason: *"General items find
machinery defects; only the family's own flow found the defect in §0.9's
argument."*

| Requirement | Why, and what fails without it |
|---|---|
| **Form 4s, from the live EDGAR flow, not a curated set** | §9.4 draws *"from the whole feed rather than hand-picked items"*. A curated set exercises the points the curator expected |
| **Spanning transaction codes**, at minimum P (open-market purchase), S (sale), A (award), M (option exercise), F (tax withholding) | Codes A, M and F are the ones that **look like purchases and are not**, and the grammar is long-only on genuine purchases. A corpus of P alone tests nothing the fence is for |
| **Including AMENDMENTS (Form 4/A)** | An amendment restates a filing already ingested. **Nothing in the eleven points addresses a restatement**, and whether one is an update or a second item is undetermined |
| **Including LATE filings**, past the two-business-day deadline | Position 5, `ingestion_lag_exceeds_window`, is the only route to a §13 row 15 observation, and row 15 is BLOCKED for want of one |
| **Including issuers whose CIK does not resolve** to a primary listing | Position 8. Foreign private issuers, recent IPOs and delisted names are where it fires |
| **Including at least one withdrawn or moved document** | Position 1, and it is the only stratum that requires the fetch itself to fail |
| **Spanning at least two calendar quarters** | Filing volume is seasonal around results, and a single-quarter sample confounds catalyst type with calendar |

### The other strata

| Stratum | Why it is separately required |
|---|---|
| **Non-filing regulatory disclosure** (RNS, ASX announcements) | §3.1 ranks sources by regulatory status; a corpus of one status cannot exercise the rank |
| **§0.7(g)'s voluntary corporate communications** | The distinction between compelled and commissioned disclosure *"exists as a machine-readable field in the feed; the paper simply never read it"* |
| **Running documents** | Position 2 has no other source |
| **Items with a catalyst resolving inside one session** | Position 10 |

### What the corpus must NOT contain, and it is a fence rather than an omission

***No agent-origin material.*** The filing flow and the discovery layer stay
separate, `CLAUDE.md` forbids the crossing, and **the reverse import fence above
is what now enforces it in code rather than in prose.**

***And nothing from `corpora/_trace_filings` may reach the discovery layer's
intake.*** That corpus is fenced at construction, at the loader and at the import
closure, and **the fence stays exactly as it is**: what phase 8 establishes is
that the filings should have been pointed at the *item* pipeline, **not that the
discovery fence should be relaxed to admit them.**

## The consequence for binding-path step 4, written into its description

**Step 4 reads: *"Run the trace harness (§9.4) to its stopping rule, including a
minimum sample of the primary catalyst family's live filing flow."***

***What phase 8 adds to it: the harness §9.4 requires is over the ITEM pipeline,
and this tree's `trace.py` traces the DISCOVERY layer.*** They are both §9.4
instruments and they are not the same exercise.

**So step 4 is not partly done. It has not been started**, and what has been run
is a different harness over a different pipeline, whose own product, §13 rows 21
and 23, is real and is not this.

*The eleven unexercised discovery-layer points still need proposals rather than
filings, which means more sweeps over more corpora. **That remains true and it
is a different sentence from step 4.***
