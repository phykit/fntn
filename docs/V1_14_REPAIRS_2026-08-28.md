### One observation on δₘᵢₙ, recorded and not acted on

**§3.6.8 defines δₘᵢₙ as *"the smallest effect worth pursuing"*.** `ROW29_REDERIVED`
derives it as `cheapest midpoint spread (7.0) + row 29's bound (8.7) = 15.7 bp`,
**which is the cost of trading**: a mechanism landing exactly on the floor nets
zero. *Worth pursuing* and *does not lose money* are different quantities, and
the implementation currently has the second under the first one's name. It moved
25.0 → 17.0 → 15.7 in a single day, always downward, each step defensible as a
cost derivation and the aggregate a 37% fall in the bar with no argument about
profit made anywhere in the chain.

**This is recorded and not repaired**, because raising δₘᵢₙ is a §0 decision and
because **row 41 is where the number it should be raised to gets argued.** A
floor that covers costs and returns nothing is admissible under a
backtesting objective and is not under a profitability one, and which objective
governs is the operator's to state.
